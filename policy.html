Privacy Policy Olympus Support Solution Limited

Last updated: January 1, 2026

At Olympus Support Solution Limited, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal, business, and institutional information you share with us through our website, digital platforms, inquiry forms, communication channels, and insurance-related services.

Short version: We collect and use information only to respond to inquiries, provide insurance brokerage support, operate our Insurance Digital Platform, improve our services, meet regulatory obligations, and protect the security and integrity of our systems.

Table of Contents

1. Information We Collect

Personal information may include your name, phone number, email address, organization, job title, location, and any details you provide when contacting Olympus Support Solution Limited.

Business and service information may include company details, insurance requirements, risk-related information, policy inquiries, partnership interests, platform integration requests, and supporting documents shared for service assessment.

Technical information may include browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, website usage activity, and other analytics data used to improve website performance, security, and user experience.

2. How We Use Information

Olympus Support Solution Limited may use collected information for lawful business, operational, regulatory, and service-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries: To communicate with you, answer questions, process requests, and provide relevant information about our services.
  • Providing services: To support insurance brokerage engagements, advisory work, digital platform onboarding, compliance workflows, and institutional partnerships.

We may also use information to improve our website, strengthen system security, maintain service records, meet legal or regulatory obligations, prevent misuse, and support internal business administration.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share relevant information where necessary and lawful, including with:

  • Licensed insurers, reinsurers, service providers, or professional partners involved in insurance placement, advisory support, claims assistance, or service delivery.
  • Regulatory authorities, government bodies, financial institutions, or compliance stakeholders where required by law, regulation, reporting obligations, or legitimate operational processes.
  • Technology, hosting, cybersecurity, analytics, and communication service providers who help us operate our website, digital platforms, systems, and business infrastructure.
  • Authorized internal teams, consultants, or advisors who require access to information for legitimate business, legal, regulatory, technical, or operational purposes.
  • Relevant parties where disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, prevent fraud, enforce policies, secure our systems, or respond to lawful requests.

4. Data Security & Retention

Olympus Support Solution Limited applies reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Our security approach may include:

  • Access controls, role-based permissions, and internal handling procedures.
  • Secure digital infrastructure, monitoring practices, and system protection measures.
  • Confidentiality practices for sensitive business, insurance, regulatory, and platform-related information.

Data retention:

  • We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
  • Some information may be retained for longer where required for legal, regulatory, audit, tax, insurance, contractual, dispute-resolution, or legitimate business purposes.
  • When information is no longer required, we may securely delete, archive, anonymize, or restrict access to it in accordance with applicable procedures.

How to contact us: You may contact Olympus Support Solution Limited through the official contact details provided on this website if you have questions about how your information is handled.

5. Your Rights & Policy Updates

You may contact us to request access, correction, update, or review of personal information you have provided to Olympus Support Solution Limited, subject to applicable laws, verification requirements, regulatory obligations, and operational limitations.

  • This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, regulatory obligations, or internal practices.
  • Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of the website after updates means you acknowledge the revised Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy is provided for general informational purposes only and does not constitute legal, regulatory, insurance, or data protection advice. Please consult a qualified professional where necessary.
